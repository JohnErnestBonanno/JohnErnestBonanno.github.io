### Hi! I'm John Bonanno. 

I’m a data-driven storyteller looking to explore new data sources and understand their implications. I'm happiest when using numbers to inform strategy’s core tenets of “where to play and how to win.”

I have about 10 years of experience in Advertising Technology spanning from Measurement & Verification (Intergral Ad Science), In-App Monetization (MoPub formerly under Twitter), and Connected Television (LG Ad Solutions). I also spent some time on Twitter's Trust and Safety team, where I both built dashboards reporting on the effectiveness of defense against inauthentic accounts during the signup process and identified signals unlikely to be performed by inauthentic accounts (at the time, most bots were unlikely to take the time to unfollow or mute other accounts). 

That being said, at my core I'm driven by interesting data sets, asking interesting questions, and sharing findings. In the words of W. Edwards Deming, "Without data, you're just another person with an opinon."

I try to post/share content on LinkedIn on topics I find interesting including:
- Data Visualization
- Design
- Interesting Data Sets

I've also had the opportunity throughout my career to write on both Measurement & Verification and Connected TV throughout my career (links)
